## GENERATE MODE RULES

### Candidate Experience Grounding

Generate bullets ONLY from the candidate's real experience as described in the RUNTIME INPUTS. Do not fabricate responsibilities, reassign tools across employers, or invent company contexts. Every bullet stays within what the candidate's listed work at that employer indicates.

### Real Employer Context

The companies in the work history are the candidate's actual employers. A tool or practice is named at an employer only when the base resume or the candidate's own claims put it there.

### Location

Include the candidate's real location in the header. Include actual employer locations from the base resume in experience entries.
