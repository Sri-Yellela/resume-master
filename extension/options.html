Resume Master — Capture Shortcut

Press a key combination to capture the job on the page you're viewing into your Resume Master job pool. The default is Ctrl+Shift+K (⌘+Shift+K on Mac).

This custom shortcut only works while a supported job page has focus (LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, Lever, Greenhouse, Workable). It cannot override browser or OS shortcuts — reserved combinations (Ctrl/Cmd+C, Ctrl/Cmd+T, devtools shortcuts, etc.) are rejected. The default Ctrl+Shift+K binding is managed by Chrome itself and can also be changed at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
