PDF tools that never upload your file

Every tool here runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored or seen by anyone — free, no account.

Merge PDFCombine PDFs into one file, in the order you choose. Split PDFSplit a PDF into single pages, or into the page ranges you choose. Organize PDFReorder, rotate or delete the pages of a PDF, with a picture of each page. Compress PDFMake a PDF smaller for an upload limit: the pictures shrink and the text stays selectable — or, at Maximum, every page becomes a picture for the smallest file. Edit PDFEdit the text of a PDF where it is — click any line and type, change its face, size and colour, and save a PDF whose old words are gone. Sign PDFSign a PDF with your signature and initials — drawn, typed, or traced from a photo into clean vector lines — and add the date. Watermark PDFStamp text diagonally across every page of a PDF. Protect PDFLock a PDF with a password, encrypted with AES-256. Unlock PDFRemove the password from a PDF you have the password for. It never guesses one. JPG to PDFTurn JPEG or PNG pictures into one PDF, a page per picture. PDF to JPGSave every page of a PDF as a picture. PDF to textCopy the text out of a PDF, in reading order — two columns read left, then right. No AI. PDF to WordTurn a PDF into an editable Word document: the text in reading order, with headings and paragraphs. PDF to ExcelTurn tables and statements in a PDF into an Excel workbook — rows and columns, numbers kept as numbers. OCR PDFRead the text in a scanned PDF, and get a searchable copy you can select and search. HTML to PDFTurn an HTML file or pasted HTML into a PDF with your browser's own printing — scripts never run and nothing is fetched.

Why it is private

Most PDF sites upload your file to their servers to work on it. These tools do the work on your own computer, in this page — so a résumé, a contract or a statement never travels anywhere.

What each conversion carries

PDF to Word keeps the text, in reading order, as headings and paragraphs — not the exact layout, fonts or pictures. PDF to Excel builds rows and columns from text that is aligned in columns. OCR reads English from scanned pages and makes a searchable copy; read it through, because OCR misreads some characters. Compress at Maximum turns every page into a picture, so its text can no longer be selected or read by software.
