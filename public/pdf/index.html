PDF tools that never upload your file

Every tool here runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored or seen by anyone — free, no account.

01 Merge PDF Combine PDFs into one file, in the order you choose. 02 Split PDF Split a PDF into single pages, or into the page ranges you choose. 03 Organize PDF Reorder, rotate or delete the pages of a PDF, with a picture of each page. 04 Compress PDF Make a PDF smaller for an upload limit: the pictures shrink, the text stays selectable and readable. 05 Watermark PDF Stamp text diagonally across every page of a PDF. 06 Protect PDF Lock a PDF with a password, encrypted with AES-256. 07 Unlock PDF Remove the password from a PDF you have the password for. It never guesses one. 08 JPG to PDF Turn JPEG or PNG pictures into one PDF, a page per picture. 09 PDF to JPG Save every page of a PDF as a picture. 10 PDF to text Copy the text out of a PDF, in reading order — two columns read left, then right. No AI.

Why it is private

Most PDF sites upload your file to their servers to work on it. These tools do the work on your own computer, in this page — so a résumé, a contract or a statement never travels anywhere.

What these tools do not do

Convert to Word or Excel, read scanned PDFs (OCR), or shrink a file by turning its pages into pictures — that would make a résumé unreadable to the systems employers use to read it.
